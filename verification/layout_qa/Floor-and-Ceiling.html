Floor and Ceiling Counties

P = Democratic primary-ballot share (proxy — Arkansas has no party registration). 2026 general-election figures are UNOFFICIAL (not yet certified statewide; Arkansas SOS results feed v234, Nov 19 2026). Governor race only. These pages measure a pattern; they make no claim about intent, fraud or cause.

How to read floor and ceiling counties

  • Ceiling counties have the five largest ΔG. All five lost Democratic Senate-primary votes, so their ΔG is mostly a fall in the proxy, not a rise in S.
  • Floor counties have the most Republican baseline: the five lowest 2022 proxy shares.
  • The lowest ΔG in the state is Pulaski (−18.14), where the proxy rose sharply.

Terminology

  • Ceiling counties: the five largest ΔG: Dallas, Sevier, St. Francis, Calhoun, Nevada.
  • Floor counties: the five lowest 2022 proxy shares P: Searcy, Newton, Cross, Prairie, Montgomery.
  • Non-loss counties: no drop in Democratic Senate-primary votes from 2022 to 2026 (52 counties). Raw numbers only.
  • Loss counties: Democratic Senate-primary votes fell (23 counties). These run the residual step.
  • Narrowed-out counties: counties left out of the narrowed fit because they distort it (Chicot, Dallas, Desha, Franklin, Jefferson, Lee, Phillips, Pulaski, Sevier, Washington).

Over-ballot check

Checks in each year: total governor votes > ballots cast; Democratic + Republican governor votes > ballots cast; ballots cast > registered voters; governor votes > registered voters; Democratic + Republican Senate-primary votes > primary ballots.

  • Yell County, 2022 (flag): 5,289 governor votes (4,172 Sanders, 1,022 Jones, 95 Harrington) against 5,033 ballots cast and 9,962 registered voters. Both passes and the published county table show the same vote counts. Every other statewide race in Yell also has more votes than 5,033, so the published ballots-cast figure looks too low rather than one race too high. Yell's 2022 S (20.31%) is computed on that ballots figure.
  • SOS bulk file (Pass A), 2022: White and Conway precinct rows also exceed their own ballots cast (see Home). These are file errors; the certified county totals do not exceed ballots.
