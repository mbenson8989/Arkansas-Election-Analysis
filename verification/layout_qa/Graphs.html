Graphs

P = Democratic primary-ballot share (proxy — Arkansas has no party registration). 2026 general-election figures are UNOFFICIAL (not yet certified statewide; Arkansas SOS results feed v234, Nov 19 2026). Governor race only. These pages measure a pattern; they make no claim about intent, fraud or cause.

Every chart carries the proxy label, the source and the 2026 UNOFFICIAL status in its footer. Bars run left to right from the largest county to the smallest (Census Vintage 2025).

1. Slope chart: G, 2022 → 2026

County G (S − P), 2022 → 2026. Red = ceiling counties; blue = floor counties.

2. ΔG by county, population order

ΔG for all 75 counties, largest to smallest population, with the ceiling and floor counties marked.

3. What ΔG is made of: ΔS vs ΔP

ΔS (change in Democratic share of ballots) and −ΔP (minus the change in the proxy) for each county. ΔG = ΔS − ΔP.

4. Democratic share of ballots and the proxy, population order

Bars: Democratic share of ballots, with the 2026 gain stacked on top. Markers: proxy P in 2022 and 2026.

5. Forward fit: Arkansas-only constants

G vs P, 2022 and 2026, with the all-county and narrowed least-squares lines. Black outline = narrowed-out counties.

6. ΔG vs ΔP

ΔG against the proxy change ΔP. Slope −0.979; the intercept (+2.09) is the typical rise in S once ΔP is removed.

7. Regional constants

Separate fits for Northwest, Central, Northeast and South, 2022 (top) and 2026 (bottom), against the statewide line.

8. Over-ballot check

Ballots cast minus (Democratic + Republican governor votes), as a share of ballots. Yell 2022 is below zero; no county is below zero in 2026.
